What we collect

  • Registration details: name, email, phone number and city.
  • Learning records: courses, lesson progress, quiz answers and scores, certificates, and questions you post.
  • Payment records: amount, method, reference numbers and uploaded receipts. Card and mobile-money details are handled by our payment provider (Chapa) and never reach us.
  • Basic technical data such as your IP address on failed log-ins, to protect accounts from password guessing.

How we use it

  • To give you access to the courses you enrolled in and track your progress.
  • To confirm payments and send you account emails (such as password resets and enrollment confirmations).
  • Your name and email are shown as a watermark on videos you watch, to discourage sharing.

Who we share it with

Only service providers needed to run the academy: our payment provider (Chapa), our video host (VdoCipher), our email provider and our hosting provider (Google Cloud). We do not sell your data.

Your choices

You can update your details on your profile page. To delete your account and data, contact us using the details below.
