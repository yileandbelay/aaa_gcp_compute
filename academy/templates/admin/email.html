Delivered, but not received?

"Sent" means the mail server accepted the message. Whether it reaches an inbox depends on your domain. Gmail, Yahoo and Outlook put mail in spam, or reject it, when the sending domain lacks SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, or when the From address is not at a domain your mail service sends for. Your mail provider's settings page shows the exact records to add in your domain's DNS. Send the test to a Gmail address as well as your own.
